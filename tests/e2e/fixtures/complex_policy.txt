CORPORATE POLICY DOCUMENT
=========================
Version 3.2 | Effective Date: January 2024

SECTION A: Data Retention Policy
--------------------------------
All customer data must be retained for a minimum of 7 years in accordance with 
federal regulations. Data should be stored in encrypted format and access 
logs must be maintained for audit purposes.

Subsection A.1: Deletion Protocols
Upon receiving a valid deletion request, data must be purged within 30 business 
days. However, backup archives may retain data for up to 90 days for disaster 
recovery purposes.

Subsection A.2: Cross-Border Data Transfer
Customer data may NOT be transferred outside the primary jurisdiction without 
explicit written consent and appropriate data protection agreements in place.

SECTION B: Employee Access Rights
---------------------------------
Employees shall have access only to data necessary for their job functions. 
All access must be logged and reviewed quarterly.

Subsection B.1: Third-Party Access
Third-party contractors may be granted temporary access for specific projects 
ONLY after signing an NDA and completing security training. Access duration 
shall not exceed 6 months without renewal.

Subsection B.2: Data Portability
Employees may request copies of their personal employment data. Such requests 
must be fulfilled within 30 days.

CONFLICT ANALYSIS (Internal Note):
---------------------------------
There exists a potential conflict between Section A and Section B regarding 
data deletion timelines. Section A requires 30-day deletion while Section B 
implies indefinite retention for audit purposes. This conflict should be 
addressed in the next policy revision.

Additionally, Section A.2 restricts cross-border transfer while certain 
third-party contractors (per B.1) may operate from international locations,
creating operational challenges for global projects.
